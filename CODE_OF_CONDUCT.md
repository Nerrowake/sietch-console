# Code of Conduct

Sietch Console follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the work. This is a community project built by and for Dune: Awakening server hosts.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a private discussion or contacting the maintainer directly via the email listed on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/michaelstoffer). All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
